## Delivery Principles

- FDEs drive the work; AI assists analysis, design, implementation, validation, documentation, and delivery.
- BMM artifacts remain the source of truth for how each workstream is delivered.
- Workstream Delivery Records summarize what the project must coordinate: status, scope edges, readiness, risks, dependencies, decisions, evidence, and next actions.
- Gaps are visible and owned; they are not hidden behind green status.

## Escalation Rules

Escalate when a workstream has:

- unresolved business decisions that block scope or acceptance
- cross-workstream dependency conflicts
- L0 contract, gate, NFR, or evidence gaps
- acceptance criteria without evidence, owner, or confirmation status
- scope change, risk acceptance, or cutover risk requiring business approval
