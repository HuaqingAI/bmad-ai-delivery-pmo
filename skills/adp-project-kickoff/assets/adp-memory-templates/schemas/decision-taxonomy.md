# Decision Taxonomy

Decisions are separated by who can make them and what project state they change.

## Types

| Type | Who Can Confirm | Destination |
| --- | --- | --- |
| FDE internal decision | FDE owner or project lead | WDR decision section or workstream decisions |
| Business decision | Business owner or delegated stakeholder | Business Decision Packet and decision log |
| Risk acceptance | Project lead plus accountable owner | Decision log and affected WDR |
| Scope change | Project lead plus business owner | Decision log, affected WDR, BMM artifact link |
| Pending clarification | The party that owns the answer | Business Decision Packet or action list |

## Business Decision Packet Shape

- Background
- Decision needed
- Options
- Recommendation
- Impacted workstreams
- Risks and trade-offs
- Deadline or trigger
- Confirming owner
- Final decision and date
